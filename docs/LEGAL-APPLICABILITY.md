# Legal applicability and source register

Review date: **16 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)**. This is research for the engine and historical BoB/Vijaya/Dena case, not a legal opinion, registration determination or public-launch clearance.

The machine-readable source of this matrix is `data/legal.json`. Each row records jurisdiction, entity/transaction scope, provision, effective date, sources, required process action and verification limit. `legal_review_needed: false` occurs only for the narrow, documented historical PSB classification.

## Findings that change product behaviour

- Classify BoB 2019 under the nationalisation Acts' statutory scheme. Keep private-bank section 44A, section 45 resolution, NBFC schemes and acquisitions as separate routes.
- Current RBI references are the entity-specific 2025 directions. The prompt's 2016/2021 starting points cannot establish current applicability.
- Keep the small-target CCI exemption unresolved after its cited March 2024 two-year period until an operative renewal is produced.
- DPDP final rules are phased. Do not represent substantive duties scheduled for May 2027 as already commenced on the review date.
- Public access and attribution do not establish redistribution rights. Commercial issuer verdicts need RA/IA classification review before launch.

## Upload and incident controls

These are engineering requirements for a hosted deployment, not a claim that this local demonstration implements a production control environment.

- Accept only explicitly supported types and sizes. Validate schema, numeric finiteness, units, reporting periods, subtotals and provenance. Keep new records unreviewed until a person confirms source/page and entity mapping.
- Treat document prose as data, never application instructions. Render labels and filenames as text; validate links against permitted HTTPS/HTTP schemes. Never fetch arbitrary uploaded URLs on the server.
- If PDF parsing is added, isolate the parser, bound CPU/time/pages/decompressed size, disable active content and quarantine unsafe files. Scanned or ambiguous tables remain manual-review items; OCR confidence alone is not reconciliation.
- Keep uploads private to the authorised user/workspace. For hosting, add authenticated access, separation between users, encryption in transit/storage, malware checks, least privilege, tested deletion and backups before accepting sensitive material.
- Do not put document contents, passwords, financial-account numbers or authentication tokens in application logs. Use event IDs and minimal metadata. Document log retention separately from document retention and applicable legal holds.
- Incident sequence: detect and record awareness time; contain access; preserve relevant evidence; identify covered incident/privacy duties; make timely notification; recover and test; record corrective action. Assign a primary and alternate incident owner before launch.
